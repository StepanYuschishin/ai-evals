04 · Control plan · M3–M5

Keep checks proportional to the risk and cost

  • Pricing: current-source numeric check plus review of unsupported terms. Investigate retrieval and source freshness.
  • API: validate meaning against specifications; exclude automated comparisons until fixed or safely routed.
  • Speed: time representative tasks. Slang warns; policy-compliant refusal passes.
  • Budget: $155,000 online; $168,250 total planned. Online cap $200,000. Weekly audits accept detection delay.
Supports a feasible control plan—not launch readiness. Confirm implementation and staffing before relying on these protections.
