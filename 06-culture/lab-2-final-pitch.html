  • Product: an AI agent that finds and explains verified market intelligence inside Ascend Analytics.
  • Problem: clients spend too long finding insights; engagement and Q4 retention are at risk.
  • Proposal: access for 50 accounts paying upwards of $50,000 annually. Decide SHIP or HOLD using the lab evidence.
DECISION REQUIRED: approve or hold this rollout. Success means fast, source-supported answers—not speed alone.
