04 · What a controlled rollout requires

Protect the client before approving an exception

  • Pricing: numeric check against the approved current reference, plus source-grounded review of billing units and conditions; rerun the corrected answer.
  • API exception: disable automated API comparisons and route those requests to human review; do not waive the unmitigated risk.
  • Speed exception: document scope, owner and timing monitoring. All Hard gates must pass before any client rollout is approved.
SHIP/HOLD impact: a documented override can narrow a Soft risk; it cannot bypass the pricing Hard failure or substitute for working controls.
